Privacy Policy

Effective 2026-10-13. This policy covers the Flow Story Maker ChatGPT plugin by Jovylle Bermudez.

Flow Story Maker is a skills-only plugin: it is a set of instructions that runs inside your own ChatGPT conversation. It has no server, no account system, no sign-in, and no analytics. It collects nothing.

collectedNothing. No accounts, no sign-in, no analytics, no cookies set by us, and no personal data transmitted to the developer.
purposeNot applicable — there is no data, so there is no use.
recipientsNone. Nothing you type into the plugin's run leaves the conversation except through ChatGPT itself, which is governed by OpenAI's own privacy policy.
retentionNone. The developer stores nothing, so there is nothing to keep or delete.
controlsStop using the plugin or uninstall it at any time — that removes everything on your side, because there is nothing on ours. To ask a question about this policy, contact us via jovylle.com.

Third parties

Your conversation runs on OpenAI's ChatGPT under OpenAI's terms and privacy policy, and the prompts the plugin writes are yours to paste into Google Flow, which is governed by Google's terms. This policy covers only what the plugin's developer receives — which is nothing.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be posted here with a new effective date.
