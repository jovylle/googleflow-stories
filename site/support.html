How to get help

Before you write

Most runs get unstuck with one of these: type .restart to start the interview over, .go to skip ahead with defaults, or .revise <item> <new value> to change one thing (for example .revise riddle … or .revise art style …) without re-planning.

What to include

  • The exact prompt or command you typed (for example .start, horror riddle story, .auto mukbang …).
  • The output you got back — paste the text, or describe which step of the run it came from (interview form, story overview, clip map, clip prompt, reference image).
  • What you expected instead, in one or two lines.

Contact

Reach the developer, Jovylle Bermudez, via jovylle.com. Once the plugin is listed, feature requests and bug reports go through its listing page — that keeps them attached to the version you are running. Until then, use the contact above, and include the exact prompt you sent and the output you got.

The rulebook source is public at github.com/jovylle/googleflow-stories; if the problem is in the documented behaviour, say which page or section you were following.
